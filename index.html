Pengyi Chen 陈鹏伊

I am a master's student in Information Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), with expected graduation in March 2027. I received my bachelor's degree in Information Engineering (IEEE Class) from SJTU in June 2024.

My research focuses on efficient inference for large language and multimodal models, including sparse attention, long-context diffusion language models, and test-time token compression. I am interested in connecting algorithm design with efficient GPU implementations.

My research experience includes an internship at Alibaba's Taobao & Tmall Group (November 2025–October 2026).
